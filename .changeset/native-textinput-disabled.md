React Native: a styled `TextInput` accepts `disabled`. With `disabled` (or `aria-disabled`), the input cannot be edited or focused and is announced as disabled, whatever `editable`, `readOnly` or `tabIndex` say, and it matches `&:disabled`. The prop is typed on `styled.TextInput` and `styled(TextInput)` from `styled-components/native`.
